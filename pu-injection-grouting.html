4-Step PU Injection Grouting Process

Precision engineering to permanently block internal water pathways.

Step 01

Leak Mapping & Diagnosis

Identifying exact crack depth, moisture trails, and structural voids using non-destructive inspection.

Step 02

Drilling & Packer Fixing

Drilling precision 10–12 mm angled holes along the crack line and locking high-pressure aluminum/steel packers.

Step 03

High-Pressure PU Injection

Pumping hydrophobic polyurethane resin deep into the RCC matrix until it expands and blocks all water flow.

Step 04

Packer Removal & Sealing

Removing packers after curing and finishing drill points with non-shrink polymer-modified repair mortar.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does high-pressure PU injection grouting stop active water leaks?

Low-viscosity hydrophobic polyurethane (PU) resin is injected under high pressure through packers directly into structural concrete cracks. Upon contact with moisture, it expands up to 20–30 times its volume into a dense, flexible waterproof foam that permanently seals water pathways.

2. Is demolition or tile breaking required for PU injection grouting?

No major demolition is required. Only small 10–12 mm holes are drilled along the leakage line to fix injection packers, making PU grouting clean, fast, and cost-effective.
