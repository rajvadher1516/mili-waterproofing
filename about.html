Our Journey

How Mili Waterproofing Began

"A building is not strong because of the cement and steel in it, but because of how well you keep water away from that steel."
— Sureshbhai, Founder
Chapter 1 · The Beginning

A Builder from the Kadiya Community

Our father, Sureshbhai, belongs to the Kadiya community, a community known for its skill in masonry and construction. Like many in the family before him, he started his working life on building sites, laying brick, casting slabs and shaping homes for Surat families.

Construction was in his hands, and in his blood.

Chapter 2 · The Problem

Strong Buildings, Weeping Walls

While building, Sureshbhai noticed something that troubled him. A house could be built perfectly, yet after a few monsoons the roof slab began to seep, walls turned damp and paint peeled away.

And the solutions available at that time were very few. Most repairs were temporary patches that failed within a season or two.

"Why should a family spend lakhs building a home and then fight water every monsoon?"

Chapter 3 · The Experiments

Trying, Failing, Trying Again

Instead of accepting the problem, he decided to solve it. On real roofs and real walls, he began to try different methods and material combinations, watching carefully what worked through the next rain, and what did not.

Not every attempt succeeded. But every failure taught him how water travels through concrete, and slowly, the leaks began to stop.

Chapter 4 · 1995

Success Spreads by Word of Mouth

One dry roof led to another. Neighbours told neighbours, and families who had given up hope called him back. As his success grew, Mili Waterproofing was officially founded in 1995, the same year his younger son Raj was born.

Hundreds of roofs, tanks and basements across Surat have been protected since.

Chapter 5 · The Next Generation

Two Sons Join the Mission

Balmukund (born 1992) and Raj (born 1995) grew up around waterproofing sites, learning the craft by watching their father work. Both chose to continue the legacy rather than leave it.

In 2017, Raj brought modern technology into the family trade: non-destructive leak detection and high-pressure PU injection, so that hidden leaks can be found without breaking tiles and active seepage can be stopped at its source.

Chapter 6 · Today

Experience of Father, Energy of Sons

Today, Balmukund and Raj work together under the Mili Waterproofing name, with their father's guidance behind every job. Traditional field wisdom meets modern diagnostics, and every client gets honest advice, careful work and a written warranty.
