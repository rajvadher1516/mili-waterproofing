What We See on Varachha Roofs

Why Terraces Leak in Varachha & Mota Varachha

Most calls we get here fall into one of these patterns. Spot yours below.

Row house roof, 15+ years old

Original coba or plaster has cracked, small gaps at the parapet let rain in, and a damp patch shows on the top-floor ceiling every monsoon.

Society terrace with many owners

Shared terraces with tanks, pipes and stairhead rooms leak at joints, and everyone below blames someone else. A written inspection finding helps the committee decide.

Top-floor flat that is too hot

Bare slab in direct sun heats the room all evening. A heat-reflective topcoat is often the cheaper fix compared with running the AC all night.

Commercial building roof

Shops, offices and small units in the Varachha market belts need a roof that stays dry, because a ceiling leak can damage stock and wiring.

Our Solutions

What We Apply on Your Terrace

The system is chosen after seeing your roof, not before.

Elastomeric Membrane on RCC

Fiber-mesh reinforced polymer coating that stretches over hairline cracks and stops standing rainwater from reaching the ceiling.

China Mosaic Joint Sealing

We keep your existing tiles, fill the hollow joints with waterproof polymer grout and finish with a seamless transparent or white protective coat.

Old Brick-Bat Coba Treatment

Soaked or loose coba is cut out where needed, the rest is sealed, and the roof is brought back to a slope that drains properly.

Heat-Reflective Cool Roof Coating

High-SRI white topcoat that lowers roof surface temperature and helps top-floor rooms stay more comfortable in Surat summers.

Parapet, Corners & Drain Outlets

V-groove crack filling and chamfer (watak) sealing along the parapet, plus sealing around drain pipes, which is where leaks often start.

Frequently Asked Questions (Varachha & Nearby Areas)

1. Do you provide same-day terrace inspection in Varachha and Mota Varachha?

Yes. Our technical team can usually reach Varachha, Mota Varachha, Sarthana, Simada, Kapodra and Punagam for a same-day inspection, subject to availability.

2. Can terrace waterproofing be done over existing China Mosaic tiles without breaking them?

Yes. In most row houses and societies across Varachha, we clean and treat hollow joints in the existing China Mosaic and apply a reinforced elastomeric membrane over it without heavy breaking.

3. My old row house terrace has a thick brick-bat coba layer. What should I do?

Old brick-bat coba holds water like a sponge once it cracks. After inspection we decide whether it can be sealed and coated, or whether loose or soaked portions must be removed first. We explain this to you before starting.

4. Terrace water leaks into the neighbour's house or the flat below. Who should waterproof?

Leaks usually come from the roof slab, shared parapet or drain above, so the fix is on the terrace. In societies, the committee normally approves a common terrace job; for a row house, the owner of the terrace slab does it. We can inspect and give a written finding to help you decide.

5. Can you waterproof the terrace of a textile or commercial building in Varachha?

Yes, for RCC terraces of shops, offices and small commercial buildings. Large or special roofs are checked on site first, and we tell you honestly if a different solution is needed.

6. When is the best time to get terrace waterproofing done in Surat?

The dry months before the monsoon are ideal because the slab is dry and curing is smooth. Work can also be done after the monsoon once the terrace has dried, but waiting for the first leak usually costs more in ceiling and paint repair.

7. Does your terrace coating also reduce top-floor summer heat?

Yes. Our high-SRI white elastomeric topcoat reflects solar radiation, lowering roof surface temperature by 5°C to 10°C during peak Surat summers.
