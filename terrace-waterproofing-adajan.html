Why Adajan & Pal Homeowners Trust Mili Waterproofing

Across Adajan, Pal, Palanpur, Jahangirpura, and Rander, high-rise residential towers, duplex bungalows, and terrace gardens face high coastal humidity near the Tapi river along with intense summer heat and heavy monsoon rain. Over time, thermal expansion creates micro-cracks in RCC roof slabs and loosens China Mosaic tile joints, causing ceiling dampness and paint flaking on upper floors.

Mili Waterproofing provides accurate on-site moisture diagnosis using non-destructive thermal scanning and applies multi-layer elastomeric polymer systems backed by an official 3 to 10-year warranty.
