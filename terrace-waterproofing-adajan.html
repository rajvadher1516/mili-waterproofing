What Causes Roof Seepage in Adajan & Pal?

Large residential slabs, landscaped terraces, and rooftop fixtures create specific trouble spots if not sealed properly.

Planters & terrace gardens

Frequent watering around pots and garden beds keeps moisture on the slab longer, finding its way through hairline cracks and outlets.

Solar stands, tanks & pipes

Roof fixtures and utility conduits introduce dozens of anchor points and joints where rainwater can slip underneath.

High afternoon heat on top slabs

Unprotected slabs in Pal and Adajan absorb heavy solar heat by afternoon, making top-floor bedrooms and living rooms warmer.

Slow drainage & open tile joints

Wind-driven rain and hollow China Mosaic joints allow water to travel sideways before showing up as dampness below.

Frequently Asked Questions (Adajan & Pal Area)

1. Do you handle waterproofing for terrace gardens and planter areas in Adajan and Pal?

Yes. Planters and pots are shifted in sections so we can treat and test the slab beneath, with extra sealing around planter bases, drain outlets, and parapet joints where moisture stays longest.

2. Is breaking existing China Mosaic or terrace tiles mandatory in Adajan apartments and bungalows?

Not usually. Where the base slab is sound, we open and seal hollow joints with waterproof polymer grout and apply a fiber-reinforced elastomeric membrane over the surface without heavy demolition.

3. Can you waterproof roofs that already have solar panels, overhead tanks, or AC stands?

Yes. We pay special attention to stand footplates, pipe penetrations, and tank plinths first, sealing vulnerable joints before applying the protective coating around mounted fixtures.

4. How much can a cool roof coating lower top-floor heat in Pal and Adajan?

A high-SRI heat-reflective white topcoat typically lowers roof surface temperature by 5°C to 10°C during peak summer afternoon sun, helping top-floor rooms retain less heat.

5. How many days does a typical residential terrace job take from start to finish?

Most bungalow or society terrace jobs take 2 to 4 days depending on terrace size, surface preparation needed, and curing time. Light foot traffic is allowed after curing, while heavy planters or furniture wait for our team's go-ahead.
