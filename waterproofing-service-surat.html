Waterproofing Service in Surat: FAQs

Which waterproofing service do I need for a leaking ceiling in Surat?

If the ceiling leaks mainly during or after rain, the cause is usually the terrace slab, parapet joints or rainwater outlets, so terrace waterproofing is needed. If it drips on dry days too, a concealed pipe or tank leak is likely and leak detection should come first.

What is the cost of waterproofing service in Surat?

Cost depends on the area in sq. ft., the condition of the slab or wall, the system used and the access at site. Mili Waterproofing gives a clear quotation after a site inspection. You can also send your measurements on WhatsApp at +91 9714343034.

Do you give a written warranty on waterproofing work?

Yes. Depending on the system used and the slab condition, a 3 to 10-year written warranty is provided covering materials and workmanship.

Can leaks be fixed without breaking tiles?

In many cases yes. Concealed pipe leaks are located with non-destructive scanning so only a small area is opened, and active seepage in walls or slabs can be stopped with PU injection grouting through small drilled holes.

Is there a food grade option for drinking water tanks?

Yes. For RCC and brick masonry tanks that store drinking water, a food grade water tank coating option is available on request. Plastic tanks are not waterproofed.
