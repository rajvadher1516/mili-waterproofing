Water Tank Waterproofing Services in Surat

Reinforced concrete (RCC) and brick masonry water storage structures, including underground sumps and rooftop overhead tanks, are essential utilities for residential societies and commercial properties across Surat. Over time, constant water pressure and structural movement cause hairline cracks and joint leaks, leading to water loss and dampness in surrounding walls or slabs. At Mili Waterproofing, we provide dedicated waterproofing solutions for RCC and brick masonry water tanks.

Common Leakage Points in Water Tanks

  • Pipe Junctions: Inlet, outlet, and overflow pipe sleeves that develop gaps over time.
  • Floor-to-Wall Corners: 90-degree bottom joints prone to stress and hairline cracking.
  • Wall & Slab Joints: Construction seams and porous concrete patches.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is food grade coating for water tanks?

It is a waterproof lining made specifically for tanks that store drinking water, helping stop leaks while keeping stored water safe for domestic use.

When should I choose food grade coating?

If your tank stores water used for drinking, cooking, or bathing, you should select the food grade coating option. For non-drinking water storage, standard tank waterproofing can be used.

Do you provide waterproofing for plastic tanks?

No. We exclusively provide waterproofing services for RCC and brick masonry water tanks (underground sumps and overhead tanks).

When can the tank be refilled after waterproofing?

Proper drying and curing time is required before refilling the tank. Our team will confirm the exact schedule at the site depending on tank condition and weather.
