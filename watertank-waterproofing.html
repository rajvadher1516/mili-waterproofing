Water Tank Waterproofing Services in Surat

Reinforced concrete (RCC) and brick masonry water storage structures—including underground sumps and rooftop overhead tanks—are essential utilities for residential societies and commercial properties across Surat. Over time, constant hydrostatic pressure and joint movements cause hairline fractures and joint leaks, resulting in water loss and dampness. At Mili Waterproofing™, we specialize in comprehensive waterproofing solutions exclusively for RCC and brick masonry water tanks.

Common Leakage Zones in Water Tanks

  • Pipe Junctions: Inlet, outlet, and overflow pipe sleeves that develop gaps due to constant vibration.
  • Floor-to-Wall Corners: 90-degree right-angle junctions prone to shear stress and cracking.
  • Construction Joints: Seams between floor slabs and vertical wall casting.
